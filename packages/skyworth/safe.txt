# Skyworth / SW (Coocaa) TV packages, also found on AcerPure TVs (Skyworth's TVs sold in India), that are content hubs, retail features or reporters. Nothing a normal TV needs.
# Names read from a Skyworth SW-22AE NF (Google TV, Android 11, MediaTek). After disabling them the home screen, Settings,
# YouTube, the AirPlay receiver and switching to HDMI 1 were checked; the tuner was NOT tested.
com.smartdevice.dailyshortcut            # Daily Hub: art / ambient and content home, runs at boot, uses location
com.smartdevice.dailymanage              # Daily Manage: content hub helper, draws over other apps
com.smartdevice.recommendation           # Daily Play: content recommendations and splash
com.smartdevice.epopplayer               # EPOP player: retail / promo content player, runs at boot
com.smartdevice.aftersale                # after-sale / service reporter
com.smartdevice.myapp                    # MyApp: the maker's own app store (use the Play Store)
com.coocaa.demonow.lightining            # retail demo mode
fusion.android.tv.demo                   # retail display demo
